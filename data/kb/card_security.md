## 怀疑银行卡被盗用时先做什么 / First steps if your card may be compromised
如果发现卡片丢失、被他人拿走，或怀疑卡资料已经泄露，请先在狮城银行 App 的“银行卡 > 冻结卡片”中冻结卡片，并通过 App 内官方客服入口报告。若怀疑登录账户或手机也被控制，请使用“设置 > 安全 > 紧急冻结”。冻结和提交报告是两个独立步骤：创建工单不会自动冻结卡片。请勿向来电者、商户或聊天对象提供 PIN、密码或 OTP；只使用自行打开的官方 App 联系银行。

If your card is missing, taken by someone else, or its details may have been exposed, freeze it in the Lion City Bank demo app under Cards > Freeze card, then report the issue through in-app support. If your login or phone may also be compromised, use Settings > Security > Emergency lock. Freezing the card and submitting a report are separate actions: creating a ticket does not freeze the card automatically. Never share your PIN, password or OTP with a caller, merchant or chat contact; contact the bank through the official app that you open yourself.

## 发现非本人银行卡交易怎么办 / What to do about an unrecognised card transaction
如果交易详情显示的商户、金额或时间无法辨认，先不要仅凭商户简称判断为盗刷；可核对交易详情、家人共用卡情况以及近期线上订阅或预授权。仍确认不是本人或授权人操作时，请立即冻结卡片并通过 App 报告，提供交易日期、金额、币种、商户显示名称和参考编号。不要在聊天中发送完整卡号、安全码或 OTP。银行会登记并核查，是否退款及处理时间需以调查结果和适用规则为准，不能仅凭提交报告承诺退款。

If the merchant, amount or time shown in a transaction is unfamiliar, do not decide it is fraud from an abbreviated merchant name alone. Check the transaction details, whether an authorised supplementary-card holder used the card, and any recent subscriptions or pre-authorisations. If you confirm that neither you nor an authorised user made it, freeze the card immediately and report it in the app with the date, amount, currency, displayed merchant name and reference number. Do not send your full card number, security code or OTP in chat. The bank will register and review the case; any refund and its timing depend on the investigation and applicable rules, so a report alone does not guarantee reimbursement.

## 小额验证扣款或陌生商户名称 / Small verification charges or unfamiliar merchant names
部分商户会先进行小额卡片验证，或在账单上使用与门店不同的公司名称；预授权也可能暂时显示为待处理。这些情况不一定代表盗刷。请点开 App 中的交易详情，查看状态、交易时间和费用说明；如商户仍无法确认，可先联系商户核实。若该笔交易不是您或授权人发起，或同时出现其他异常交易，请冻结卡片并报告，不要等待商户答复后才采取安全措施。

Some merchants make a small card verification charge or use a billing-company name different from the shop name; a pre-authorisation may also appear as pending temporarily. These situations do not by themselves prove fraud. Open the transaction details in the app and check its status, date and fee description; if the merchant is still unclear, you may ask the merchant to identify it. If neither you nor an authorised user initiated the charge, or there are other suspicious transactions, freeze the card and report them without waiting for the merchant's reply.

## ATM 已扣款但没有收到现金 / ATM debited the account but dispensed no cash
如果 ATM 没有吐钞但账户显示扣款，请记录取款时间、金额、ATM 所在地点或编号，并保存交易通知或收据。先查看交易是待处理还是已入账；不要为了验证而连续重复取款。如果使用的是狮城银行 ATM，可通过 App 报告“ATM 未吐钞”；如果使用其他银行的 ATM，也请向发卡银行报告并提供 ATM 运营方信息。若交易不是您发起的，按非本人交易处理，立即冻结卡片并报告。调查结果和冲正时间以银行核查为准。

If an ATM did not dispense cash but your account shows a debit, note the time, amount and ATM location or identifier, and keep any notification or receipt. Check whether the transaction is pending or posted; do not make repeated withdrawals just to test it. For a Lion City Bank demo ATM, report “ATM cash not dispensed” in the app. For another bank's ATM, report it to your card issuer and provide the ATM operator details. If you did not initiate the transaction, treat it as an unrecognised transaction, freeze the card and report it immediately. The investigation outcome and reversal timing depend on the bank's review.

## 本人操作但对交易金额或扣款有异议 / Disputing an authorised transaction amount or charge
如果您确认自己做过这笔交易，但金额不符、重复扣款、商品或服务未提供，或退款迟迟未显示，这属于交易争议，不等同于盗刷。通常先向商户索取订单、退款或更正记录；同时保存收据、订单、与商户沟通记录及交易参考编号。若无法解决，可在 App 中选择“申请交易核查”提交争议。银行可能需要商户凭证并按卡组织流程调查；待处理交易、退款显示时间和可申请期限各有不同，提交申请不代表争议一定成立或款项一定退回。

If you confirm that you made the transaction but the amount is wrong, it was charged more than once, goods or services were not provided, or a refund has not appeared, this is a transaction dispute rather than card fraud. Usually ask the merchant first for an order, refund or correction record. Keep receipts, order details, correspondence with the merchant and the transaction reference. If it remains unresolved, submit a dispute using “Request transaction review” in the app. The bank may need evidence from the merchant and investigate under card-network procedures. Pending transactions, refund posting times and filing time limits vary; submitting a claim does not mean it will be accepted or refunded.

## 如何查询盗刷或交易争议工单 / Checking a fraud or transaction-dispute ticket
提交报告或申请核查后，可在“我的工单”查看工单编号、当前状态、更新时间和坐席回复。若状态仍为“待处理”，表示尚未完成核查；不要重复提交相同申请。需要补充资料时，请通过 App 显示的工单入口提交，不要把卡片密码、PIN、CVV 或 OTP 发给任何人。工单状态更新不代表卡片已冻结、交易已撤销或退款已批准；这些结果应分别在卡片状态和交易详情中确认。

After reporting fraud or requesting a review, open My Tickets to see the ticket number, current status, last update and agent response. “Pending” means the review is not complete; avoid filing the same request again. If more information is needed, submit it through the ticket entry shown in the app. Never send your card password, PIN, CVV or OTP to anyone. A ticket update does not mean the card is frozen, the transaction reversed or a refund approved; check the card status and transaction details separately.
