## 同一笔银行卡消费显示两次 / The same card purchase appears twice
先打开两条记录，比较商户、金额、时间及状态。商户有时会先显示待处理授权，之后才显示最终入账；待处理记录可能在商户完成结算或取消后变化。若两笔都已入账，或其中一笔在合理等待后仍未消失，请联系商户确认是否重复请款，并通过 App 申请交易核查。保留两笔记录和商户答复；不要仅凭两条记录都出现过就假定会自动退款。

Open both entries and compare the merchant, amount, time and status. A merchant may show a pending authorisation before the final posting; the pending entry can change after the merchant completes or cancels settlement. If both entries are posted, or one remains after a reasonable wait, ask the merchant whether it submitted a duplicate charge and request a review in the app. Keep both entries and the merchant's response; do not assume that seeing two entries automatically means a refund will follow.

## 商户说已退款但账单还没显示 / A merchant says a refund was issued but it is not on the statement
请向商户索取退款日期、金额和退款参考编号，并确认退款退回到原付款卡或原付款方式。然后在 App 查看原交易和后续账单；退款显示时间会受商户提交、卡组织和入账流程影响。若商户确认已退款但经过其告知的处理时间仍未显示，可通过 App 提交原交易编号及退款凭证，请银行核查。请勿提供完整卡号或验证码，也不要把尚未到账的退款当作已可用余额。

Ask the merchant for the refund date, amount and reference, and confirm it was sent to the original payment card or method. Check the original transaction and later statements in the app; posting time depends on the merchant, card network and settlement process. If the merchant confirms the refund but it is still missing after the timeframe they provided, submit the original transaction reference and refund evidence through the app for review. Do not share your full card number or OTP, and do not treat a refund that has not posted as available funds.

## 商户扣款金额与收据不同 / The card charge differs from the receipt
先比较收据和银行交易详情中的币种、金额及交易日期。餐饮、酒店、租车或加油等场景可能涉及小费、押金或预授权后再按最终金额结算；外币消费也可能按入账时适用的汇率换算。若最终入账金额仍与商户凭证不符，请先要求商户解释并保存收据、订单及汇率/费用明细，再通过 App 申请核查。银行不会只凭商户名称推断具体收费原因。

Compare the currency, amount and date on the receipt with the bank transaction details. Restaurants, hotels, car rentals and petrol stations may involve tips, deposits or a pre-authorisation followed by settlement for the final amount; foreign-currency purchases may also be converted using the applicable posting rate. If the posted amount still differs from the merchant record, ask the merchant to explain and keep the receipt, order and rate or fee details before requesting a review in the app. The bank cannot determine the exact reason from the merchant name alone.

## 申请交易核查需要准备什么 / Information to prepare for a transaction review
请准备交易日期、金额、币种、账单显示的商户名称和交易参考编号，并简要说明您与商户沟通过什么、希望银行核查什么。视争议类型，可附订单、收据、取消或退款确认、商户往来记录等相关材料。只通过狮城银行演示 App 的工单入口提交必要资料；不要提交密码、PIN、完整卡号、CVV 或 OTP。材料清单和可受理期限会因交易类型及卡组织规则而不同，遇到期限问题应尽快联系银行确认。

Prepare the transaction date, amount, currency, merchant name as shown on the statement and transaction reference, plus a brief description of your contact with the merchant and what you want reviewed. Depending on the dispute, relevant evidence may include an order, receipt, cancellation or refund confirmation, and merchant correspondence. Submit only necessary information through the Lion City Bank demo app's ticket channel; do not provide a password, PIN, full card number, CVV or OTP. Evidence requirements and filing windows vary by dispute type and card-network rules, so contact the bank promptly if you are unsure about a deadline.

## 先判断是盗刷还是商户争议 / First distinguish fraud from a merchant dispute
如果您没有授权或参与这笔交易，或卡片遗失后出现交易，应按疑似盗刷处理：立即冻结受影响的卡片并报告银行。若您本人做过交易，但遇到重复扣款、金额不符、退款未到账或商品服务问题，则属于商户/账单争议；通常先向商户核对并保留沟通凭证，再向银行提交核查。两类申请所需材料、受理条件和办理期限可能不同。不要为了“选一个最容易退款的理由”错误描述事实，也不要把银行受理申请理解为已批准退款。

If you did not authorise or take part in a transaction, or a transaction appears after your card was lost, treat it as suspected fraud: freeze the affected card and report it to the bank immediately. If you made the purchase but there is a duplicate charge, wrong amount, missing refund or a problem with goods or services, it is a merchant or billing dispute; usually contact the merchant first, keep the evidence and then ask the bank to review it. Evidence, eligibility and time limits can differ between the two routes. Describe what happened accurately rather than choosing a reason to improve the chance of a refund, and do not treat acceptance of a claim as approval of a refund.

## 待处理交易和已入账交易有什么不同 / Pending authorisation versus a posted transaction
“待处理”通常表示商户已取得授权，资金或额度暂时被占用，但最终金额尚未作为正式交易入账。商户可能完成请款、调整金额或取消授权；银行能否在这个阶段受理争议取决于交易类型和处理规则。请查看 App 的状态并向商户确认；若最终入账仍有问题，再按盗刷或商户争议的实际情况提交核查。如果怀疑卡片已被盗用，不要等它入账才冻结卡片或报告。预授权释放和入账时间没有适用于所有商户的统一期限。

“Pending” generally means the merchant obtained an authorisation and funds or credit are temporarily held, but the final transaction has not yet posted. The merchant may complete settlement, change the amount or cancel the authorisation; whether a dispute can be accepted at this stage depends on the transaction and applicable rules. Check the app status and ask the merchant; if a problem remains after posting, submit the appropriate fraud report or merchant dispute. If you suspect your card has been compromised, do not wait for posting before freezing or reporting it. There is no single release or posting timeframe that applies to every merchant.
