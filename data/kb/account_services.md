## 开户条件 / Who can open an account
<!-- sub_intents: age_limit -->
年满 16 岁的新加坡公民、永久居民，以及持有效准证（如就业准证、学生准证）的外国人，都可以开立储蓄账户。16 岁以下需由父母或监护人开立联名账户。开立往来账户和信用卡需年满 21 岁，信用卡另需满足最低收入要求。

Singapore citizens, permanent residents and foreigners holding a valid pass (such as an Employment Pass or Student's Pass) aged 16 or above can open a savings account. Under 16, a parent or guardian must open a joint account. Current accounts and credit cards require you to be 21 or above; credit cards also have a minimum income requirement.

## 身份验证 / Verifying your identity
<!-- sub_intents: verify_my_identity, why_verify_identity -->
网上开户可以通过 Singpass（MyInfo）自动填写并验证资料，几分钟内完成。没有 Singpass 的外国人可上传护照和准证，再拍摄一张自拍完成人脸比对。根据新加坡金融管理局（MAS）关于反洗钱和反恐融资的规定，银行必须核实每位客户的身份，并在资料变更或定期复核时重新验证。

When you open an account online, Singpass (MyInfo) fills in and verifies your details in minutes. Foreigners without Singpass can upload a passport and pass, then take a selfie for face matching. Under the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) rules on anti-money laundering and countering the financing of terrorism, we must verify every customer's identity, and verify it again when details change or during periodic reviews.

## 身份验证失败 / When identity verification fails
<!-- sub_intents: unable_to_verify_identity -->
验证失败的常见原因：证件照片模糊或反光、证件已过期、自拍光线太暗、填写的姓名与证件不一致。请在光线充足处重新拍摄并确认证件在有效期内。连续 3 次失败后，请携带证件到任何分行办理，柜台会人工核实。

Common reasons verification fails: the document photo is blurry or has glare, the document has expired, the selfie is too dark, or the name entered does not match the document. Retake the photos in good light and check that your document is still valid. After 3 failed attempts, visit any branch with your documents and a staff member will verify you in person.

## 修改个人资料 / Updating your personal details
<!-- sub_intents: edit_personal_details -->
地址、手机号码和电子邮箱可以在 App 的"设置 > 个人资料"中修改，需要短信验证码（OTP）确认；修改手机号码时，验证码会同时发送到新旧号码。如果已绑定 Singpass，地址会自动同步。修改姓名或证件号码需携带证明文件（如结婚证书或新证件）到分行办理。

You can change your address, mobile number and email in the app under Settings > Personal details, confirmed with a one-time password (OTP); when changing your mobile number, codes are sent to both the old and new numbers. If you use Singpass, your address updates automatically. To change your name or ID number, visit a branch with supporting documents such as a marriage certificate or new ID.

## 忘记登录密码 / Forgotten login password
<!-- sub_intents: passcode_forgotten -->
在 App 或网上银行登录页面点击"忘记密码"，输入 NRIC 或护照号码以及注册手机号，完成短信验证码验证后即可设置新密码。连续输错密码 5 次后账户登录会被锁定 24 小时，也可以按上述步骤重设密码立即解锁。狮城银行不会通过电话或短信向您索取密码或验证码。

Tap "Forgot password" on the app or internet banking login page, enter your NRIC or passport number and registered mobile number, confirm with the OTP, and set a new password. After 5 wrong passwords in a row, login is locked for 24 hours; resetting the password as above unlocks it immediately. Lion City Bank will never ask for your password or OTP by phone or SMS.

## 销户 / Closing your account
<!-- sub_intents: terminate_account -->
储蓄账户可以在 App 的"设置 > 关闭账户"中申请关闭，或到分行办理。关闭前请先把余额转出、取消 GIRO 和其他自动扣款，并还清关联信用卡的欠款。销户一般在 7 个工作日内完成，开户 6 个月内销户收取 SGD 30。

You can close a savings account in the app under Settings > Close account, or at a branch. Before closing, transfer out the balance, cancel GIRO and other automatic payments, and pay off any linked credit card balance. Closure usually takes 7 working days. Closing an account within 6 months of opening costs SGD 30.

## 多币种账户支持的货币 / Currencies in the multi-currency account
<!-- sub_intents: fiat_currency_support -->
多币种账户支持 11 种货币：SGD、USD、CNY、MYR、EUR、GBP、AUD、JPY、HKD、THB 和 IDR。可以持有、兑换、收取和汇出这些货币。本行不支持任何加密货币的存放、兑换或转账。

The multi-currency account supports 11 currencies: SGD, USD, CNY, MYR, EUR, GBP, AUD, JPY, HKD, THB and IDR. You can hold, convert, receive and send these currencies. We do not support holding, converting or transferring any cryptocurrency.

## 存款保险 / Deposit insurance
狮城银行是新加坡存款保险计划的成员。根据新加坡存款保险公司（SDIC）的规定，每位存款人在同一家银行的新元存款最多受保 SGD 100,000，储蓄账户、往来账户和定期存款里的新元合起来计算，不是每个账户各保 SGD 100,000。外币存款（包括多币种账户里的外币）、结构性存款和投资产品不在保障范围内。存款保险自动生效，客户不需要申请，也不用自己付费。

Lion City Bank is a member of Singapore's Deposit Insurance Scheme. Under the Singapore Deposit Insurance Corporation (SDIC) rules, each depositor's Singapore-dollar deposits at one bank are insured up to SGD 100,000 in total. Savings, current and fixed deposit balances in SGD are added together; the limit is not per account. Foreign currency deposits (including foreign currencies in a multi-currency account), structured deposits and investment products are not covered. Coverage is automatic: you do not need to apply or pay for it.

## 紧急冻结账户 / Emergency lock for all accounts
如果怀疑账户被别人盗用，比如点了可疑链接、把验证码告诉了别人，或者手机被远程控制，可以在 App 的"设置 > 安全 > 紧急冻结"一键冻结，也可以拨打 24 小时客服热线。冻结后，所有账户的网上转账、借记卡和信用卡交易、ATM 取款都会马上停止。解冻需要本人带证件到分行，或者打客服热线完成身份验证后申请。这个功能是按新加坡金融管理局（MAS）的防诈骗要求提供的。

If you think someone else is using your account, for example after tapping a suspicious link, sharing an OTP or finding your phone remotely controlled, use Settings > Security > Emergency lock in the app, or call our 24-hour hotline. The lock immediately stops online transfers, debit and credit card transactions and ATM withdrawals on all your accounts. To unlock, visit a branch with your ID, or call the hotline and pass identity verification. We provide this feature under the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) anti-scam requirements.

## 资金锁 / Money Lock
资金锁可以把储蓄账户里的一部分钱锁起来，锁住的钱不能通过网上银行或 App 转出，就算手机或账户被盗用也转不走。可以在 App 的"账户 > 资金锁"设置，最少锁 SGD 1,000。锁住的钱还在原来的账户里，余额照常显示，利息照常计算。解锁只能由本人带证件到分行办理，不能在网上解锁。

Money Lock sets aside part of your savings account balance so it cannot be transferred out through internet banking or the app, even if your phone or account is compromised. Set it up in the app under Accounts > Money Lock, with a minimum of SGD 1,000. Locked money stays in the same account, still shows in your balance and still earns interest. To unlock it, you must visit a branch in person with your ID; it cannot be unlocked online.
