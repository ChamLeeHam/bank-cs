## 本地转账费用 / Local transfer fees
<!-- sub_intents: transfer_fee_charged -->
通过 PayNow、FAST 或 GIRO 进行新加坡本地转账免费，网上银行和 App 均不收手续费。在分行柜台办理本地转账每笔收取 SGD 5。

Local transfers by PayNow, FAST or GIRO are free in internet banking and the app. A local transfer made at a branch counter costs SGD 5 per transaction.

## 海外电汇（TT）手续费 / Overseas telegraphic transfer (TT) fees
<!-- sub_intents: transfer_fee_charged -->
每笔汇往海外的电汇（telegraphic transfer / TT）均收取 SGD 10 本行手续费，费用不因收款国家或地区（例如马来西亚）而改变。中转行和收款行可能另外扣取费用（通常相当于 SGD 10–30），收款人实际收到的金额可能因此减少。如需由汇款人承担全部费用（OUR），每笔另加 SGD 20，收款人可收到全额。

Each overseas telegraphic transfer (TT), including a TT to Malaysia, costs SGD 10 in Lion City Bank's fee. This fee does not vary by destination country. Intermediary and receiving banks may deduct their own charges (usually the equivalent of SGD 10–30), so the recipient may receive slightly less. To pay all charges yourself (OUR) so the recipient gets the full amount, add SGD 20 per transfer.

## 汇率如何确定 / How exchange rates are set
<!-- sub_intents: exchange_rate -->
外币兑换、海外电汇和多币种账户转换使用交易当时的狮城银行牌价。工作日牌价约每 5 分钟更新一次；周末和公众假期使用锁定牌价，并加收 0.5% 的波动缓冲。实时牌价可在 App 的"汇率"页面查看。

Currency conversion, overseas TTs and multi-currency account transfers use Lion City Bank's board rate at the time of the transaction. On business days the rate updates about every 5 minutes. On weekends and public holidays a locked rate applies, with a 0.5% buffer for market movement. You can see live rates on the Rates page of the app.

## 外币兑换与外币刷卡费用 / Currency conversion and foreign currency card fees
<!-- sub_intents: exchange_charge, card_payment_fee_charged -->
在多币种账户内兑换货币不另收手续费，成本已包含在买卖汇率差价中。用 Visa 或 Mastercard 卡进行外币消费（包括在海外网站以外币付款），收取交易金额 3.25% 的外币交易费；银联（UnionPay）卡为 1%。以新元付款的海外商户也可能自行收取动态货币转换（DCC）费用，建议选择以当地货币付款。

Converting currencies inside a multi-currency account has no separate fee; the cost is included in the buy and sell rates. Card payments in a foreign currency, including on overseas websites, carry a 3.25% foreign transaction fee on Visa and Mastercard, and 1% on UnionPay. Overseas merchants that charge you in SGD may add their own dynamic currency conversion (DCC) fee, so we recommend paying in the local currency.

## ATM 取款费用 / ATM withdrawal fees
<!-- sub_intents: cash_withdrawal_charge -->
在狮城银行 ATM 取款免费。在新加坡其他银行的 ATM 取款每次收取 SGD 2。在海外 ATM 取款每次收取 SGD 5，外币金额另加 3.25% 外币交易费；海外 ATM 运营商也可能另收费用，屏幕上会显示。

Withdrawals at Lion City Bank ATMs are free. Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in Singapore cost SGD 2 each. Overseas ATM withdrawals cost SGD 5 each plus the 3.25% foreign transaction fee; the overseas ATM operator may add its own fee, which is shown on the screen.

## 账单上看不懂的费用 / Unfamiliar charges on your statement
<!-- sub_intents: extra_charge_on_statement, card_payment_fee_charged -->
账单上常见的小额费用包括：外币交易费（3.25%）、海外 ATM 取款费（SGD 5）、信用卡年费、补卡费（SGD 15）以及逾期还款费。App 中点击该笔交易可以查看费用类型。如果是您完全没有做过的交易，请立即冻结卡片并举报盗刷；如果认为费用收错了，可以申请人工客服核查。

Common small charges on a statement include the foreign transaction fee (3.25%), overseas ATM fee (SGD 5), credit card annual fee, card replacement fee (SGD 15) and late payment fee. Tap the transaction in the app to see the fee type. If you never made the transaction, freeze your card and report it as fraud straight away. If you think a fee was charged by mistake, ask for a human agent to review it.

## 银行卡年费与其他卡费 / Card annual fees and other card fees
<!-- sub_intents: card_payment_fee_charged -->
借记卡没有年费。信用卡年费为 SGD 196.20，首两年免收；之后每年消费满 SGD 10,000 可申请豁免。信用卡逾期还款费为 SGD 100。借记卡或信用卡补卡费为 SGD 15；如果卡片被盗并已报警，补卡免费。

Debit cards have no annual fee. The credit card annual fee is SGD 196.20, waived for the first two years; after that it can be waived if you spend SGD 10,000 in the year. The credit card late payment fee is SGD 100. Replacing a debit or credit card costs SGD 15, or is free if the card was stolen and you have a police report.

## 外币消费为什么显示为新元 / Why a foreign-currency purchase appears in SGD
<!-- sub_intents: exchange_charge, extra_charge_on_statement -->
海外或外币网购通常会在信用卡账单上换算成新元；汇率会因日期和发卡机构而异，换算中也可能包含货币转换或行政费用。查看账单时，可对照交易日期、原始币种和金额、商户名称及本行显示的汇率或费用明细。新加坡通用说明不能确定狮城银行演示产品对某一笔交易收取了什么费用；具体金额请查看该笔交易详情或要求人工核查。如果这笔交易不是你本人授权的，请立即联系银行报告。

Overseas and other foreign-currency purchases are generally converted to SGD on a credit-card statement. The exchange rate can vary by date and issuer, and the conversion may include currency-conversion or administrative fees. When reviewing a charge, compare the transaction date, original currency and amount, merchant name, and the rate or fee details shown by your bank. General Singapore guidance cannot identify the fee applied to a particular Lion City Bank demo transaction; check that transaction's details or request a human review. If you did not authorise the transaction, contact your bank immediately to report it.

## 信用卡应还全额还是最低还款额 / Pay the full credit-card balance or only the minimum
<!-- sub_intents: card_payment_fee_charged -->
如果经济情况允许，按到期日付清本期账单通常可避免未偿余额继续产生利息。只付最低还款额并不会清偿全部欠款，剩余余额可能继续计息；利息如何计算、是否有免息期及最低还款额是多少，都以发卡机构的条款和账单为准。若暂时无法全额还款，请尽早联系发卡机构了解可用安排。

If you can afford to, paying the statement balance in full by the due date generally avoids interest continuing to accrue on an unpaid balance. Paying only the minimum does not clear the debt; the remaining balance may continue to accrue interest. The interest calculation, any interest-free period and minimum payment are set out in the issuer's terms and statement. If you cannot pay in full, contact the issuer early to discuss available arrangements.

## 海外刷卡时选择新元还是当地货币 / Choose SGD or local currency overseas
<!-- sub_intents: exchange_charge, card_payment_fee_charged -->
海外商户或 ATM 可能提供动态货币转换（DCC），让你选择以新元或当地货币结算。DCC 汇率和费用由商户或其服务商设定，可能与发卡机构的换汇成本不同；选择当地货币也不代表一定没有发卡机构外币交易费。付款前比较屏幕上显示的金额和汇率，并查看自己的银行卡条款；不清楚时不要匆忙确认。

An overseas merchant or ATM may offer dynamic currency conversion (DCC), letting you pay in SGD or the local currency. The merchant or its provider sets the DCC exchange rate and charges, which may differ from the card issuer's conversion costs. Choosing local currency does not necessarily remove the issuer's foreign-currency fee. Compare the amount and rate shown on screen and check your card terms; do not rush if the choice is unclear.

## 信用卡与借记卡的区别 / Difference between credit and debit cards
<!-- sub_intents: card_payment_fee_charged, extra_charge_on_statement -->
借记卡消费通常直接从关联存款账户扣款；信用卡消费则记入信用账户，之后需要按账单还款，未还余额可能产生利息或费用。退款、预授权、外币转换和争议处理的时间可能因卡组织、商户和发卡机构而不同。具体交易记录应以银行 App 和卡片条款为准。

Debit-card purchases are generally taken from the linked deposit account. Credit-card purchases are charged to a credit account and must be repaid according to the statement; an unpaid balance may incur interest or fees. The timing of refunds, pre-authorisations, currency conversion and disputes can vary by card network, merchant and issuer. Check the bank app and card terms for a specific transaction.
