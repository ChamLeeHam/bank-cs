# Maybank Singapore: Card-dispute reference

Source institution: Maybank Singapore Limited. Source materials checked 2026-11-15. The official dispute declaration form available through Maybank is marked **NOV2018**. This document summarizes that dated form for research; verify that Maybank still accepts it and recheck all time limits, fees, and submission channels before use. It belongs only in `bank_pdf_docs`, not the Lion City Bank customer FAQ.

## Dispute categories in the dated declaration form

The form groups cases into unauthorized transactions, duplicate billing, goods or services not received, goods received but defective or not as described, refund or credit not processed, cancelled reservations, cancelled recurring payments, incorrect amount or currency, payment by another method, and other reasons. It asks the customer to select a dispute reason and provide the supporting items listed for that category.

The currently accessible form used for this summary is marked **NOV2018**. Treat its categories as a historical reference and confirm Maybank's current form and rules before giving customer guidance.

For unauthorized transactions, the form says the card needs to be blocked. For merchant-related cases, it commonly asks for evidence such as the order or invoice, delivery or cancellation details, a credit note, and proof that the customer tried to resolve the issue with the merchant. Requirements vary by reason; the summary is not a substitute for the current form.

## Dated terms must not be presented as current policy

The NOV2018 form describes an investigation period of up to 90 calendar days or longer, and says recovery is not guaranteed. It also describes a sales-draft retrieval fee in certain cases where the transaction is determined to have been authorized. These are statements from the dated form only; do not quote their amounts or timelines as current Maybank policy without checking a current official source.

The form's submission details include mail/fax and a contact address. As these can change, direct customers to Maybank's current official website or the contact details on their card rather than copying those details into customer-facing answers.
