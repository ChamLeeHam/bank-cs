# UBS Switzerland: Card-dispute reference

Source institution: UBS Switzerland AG. Official English-language sources checked 2026-11-15. The page and forms concern Swiss card products and CHF context. This concise summary is for comparative research in `bank_pdf_docs`, not a customer instruction for Lion City Bank.

## Select the card and dispute reason

UBS directs customers to first identify whether the transaction was on a credit/prepaid card or debit card, then use the corresponding dispute path. Its credit-card page offers distinct reasons and forms for lost or stolen cards, unrecognized transactions, duplicate charges, cancelled subscriptions, goods or services not received, returned or cancelled purchases, missing credits, payment by another method, incorrect amount, cancelled hotel bookings, cash not received, and card surcharges. The page describes selecting one reason per form.

## Posting state and urgent card security

UBS says a lost or stolen card should be blocked immediately. Its page says a duplicate charge can be challenged once the amount has been definitively debited; a reserved amount may still be processing and can differ from the final amount. This distinction can inform a generic explanation of pending versus posted transactions, but the timing and procedure stated here are UBS-specific.

Official source: https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/help/creditcard/notify-abuse.html

## Swiss time limits and channels

The UBS page says statement discrepancies should be reported in writing within 30 days of the billing date, and lists UBS Switzerland channels for the forms. Those dates, contact details, and procedures must not be reused for another country or bank without checking its own current official rules.

Official source: https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/help/creditcard/notify-abuse.html
